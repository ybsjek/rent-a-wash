Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about our rentals, repairs, delivery, and service area.

Where does Rent-A-Wash provide service?

We serve Kennewick, Richland, and Pasco in Tri-Cities, plus Benton City, Hermiston, Walla Walla, College Place, and Umatilla. Contact us to ask about nearby locations.

Do you offer top load and front load rentals?

Yes. Top load and front load washer and dryer rental options are available. Contact us to confirm current availability.

How much do rentals cost?

Top load rentals are $25 every two weeks. Front load rentals are $40 every two weeks. Delivery charges and repair pricing are separate.

Do you offer flexible cancellations?

Yes. Our rentals offer flexible cancellations. Contact us to discuss cancellation and return arrangements before starting your rental.

Is affordable delivery available?

Yes. Affordable delivery is available for rentals. Contact us to confirm delivery costs and scheduling for your location.

Do you repair washers and dryers?

Yes. We offer washer and dryer repair services. Call 509-212-7621 to discuss the appliance, the problem, and next steps.

How do I apply for a rental?

Visit our application page to submit your information. We’ll follow up to discuss your application and availability.
