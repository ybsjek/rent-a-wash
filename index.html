Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about our washer and dryer rentals, repairs, delivery, and service area.

Where does Rent-A-Wash provide service?

We serve Richland, Kennewick, Pasco, Benton City, Yakima, Walla Walla, and College Place in Washington, plus Hermiston and Umatilla in Oregon. Contact us to confirm rental delivery or repair availability for your address.

Can I rent just a washer?

Yes. Washer-only rental is $25 every two weeks. This option includes one washer and does not include a dryer.

Do you offer top load and front load sets?

Yes. Top load and front load washer and dryer sets are available. Contact us to confirm current availability.

How much do washer and dryer rentals cost?

Washer-only rental is $25 every two weeks. Top load washer and dryer sets start at $35 every two weeks. Front load sets start at $45 every two weeks. Delivery is separate.

Do you offer flexible cancellations?

Yes. Our rentals offer flexible cancellations. Contact us to discuss cancellation and return arrangements before starting your rental.

Local rental delivery is $49, separate from your rental rate. Contact us to confirm coverage for your address and arrange scheduling.

Do you repair washers and dryers?

Yes. We offer washer and dryer repair services. Call 509-212-7621 to discuss the appliance, the problem, and next steps. Repair pricing is separate.
