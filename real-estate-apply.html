Homebuyer Program Application

Apply for the Rent-A-Wash Homebuyer Program through your partner real estate agent’s referral.

Getting Started

Complete the dedicated Homebuyer Program form below and include your referring real estate agent’s name.

The program requires an initial three-month rental lease. Qualifying referrals receive free delivery in Pasco, Richland, or Kennewick. Regular rental rates apply.

Longevity discounts apply only to the purchase price: 10% at six months and 25% at twelve months. They do not reduce rental rates, and rental payments do not count toward a purchase.

Review program details and pricing before submitting your application.
