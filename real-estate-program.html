Purchase Discounts Only—Not Rental Discounts

The 10% discount for a completed 6-month contract and the 25% discount for a completed 12-month contract apply only to the purchase price of the machines. They do not reduce your rental rate. Rental payments do not count toward the purchase price. This is not a rent-to-own program.

Step 3: End of Contract

Once your selected 3-, 6-, or 12-month contract is complete, choose the next step that works for your household.

Keep Renting

Continue renting month to month. Contact us before your contract ends to confirm your ongoing rental rate and billing arrangements.

Purchase Your Machines

Purchase your set outright. Base purchase prices apply after a 3-month contract. Receive 10% off the purchase price at the end of a 6-month contract or 25% off at the end of a 12-month contract.

Return the Machines

Return your machines after completing your selected contract term. Pickup is available for an additional charge. Call for pickup pricing and scheduling.
