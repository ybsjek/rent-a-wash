How the Homebuyer Program Works

Start with a referral from your partner real estate agent, then choose a rental setup for your new home.

1. Apply with Your Agent’s Name

2. Confirm Your Rental & Delivery

We’ll follow up to confirm your referral, appliance availability, rental rate, and delivery details. Sign a three-month lease to receive the program’s delivery offer.

3. Choose Your Next Step

At the end of the initial three months, continue renting month to month, purchase the machines outright, or arrange to return them.

Three Options After Your First Three Months

Your needs may change as you settle in. At the end of your initial lease, choose the option that fits your home and budget.

Keep Renting Month to Month

Continue using the machines under a month-to-month rental arrangement. Contact us to confirm the ongoing rate, billing schedule, and rental terms.

Purchase the Machines Outright

Want to keep the machines as your own? Ask us for their outright purchase price. The purchase is separate from your rental: rental payments do not reduce the purchase price.

Return the Machines

Finished renting? You may return the machines at the end of the three-month lease. Affordable pickup is available for an additional charge. Contact us to confirm the price and coordinate your return.

Homebuyer Program Questions

Details about referrals, delivery, rental terms, and your options after the initial lease.

Who is this program for?

This program is for people who recently purchased a home and were referred to Rent-A-Wash by a participating partner real estate agent.

How do I include my agent’s referral?

On our rental application, select the real estate agent referral option and enter your agent’s name. We’ll follow up to confirm the program details and availability.

How long is the initial lease?

The program requires an initial three-month rental lease. Review the lease terms before signing, including the requirements if your plans change before the initial term ends.

Are the rental rates discounted?

No. Regular rental rates apply. The program provides free delivery in Pasco, Richland, or Kennewick, or discounted delivery outside those cities within our service area.

What happens after three months?

You can continue renting month to month, purchase the machines outright, or return them. Contact us to arrange your preferred next step.

Do rental payments count toward buying the machines?

No. Rental payments pay for use of the machines during your rental period. They do not count toward or reduce the outright purchase price. This is not a rent-to-own program.

What is the purchase price?

Contact Rent-A-Wash for the purchase price of your machines. The purchase price is quoted separately from your rental payments.

Is pickup free if I return the machines?

No. Affordable pickup is available for an additional charge. Contact us to confirm pickup pricing and arrange your return.

What if my new home is outside the three cities?

Discounted delivery is available outside Pasco, Richland, and Kennewick within our service area. Call with your address to confirm coverage and your delivery price.

Do I need a separate application?

No. Use our regular rental application and include your real estate agent’s referral information.
